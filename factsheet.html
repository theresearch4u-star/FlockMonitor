Locality snapshot

Tasmania — King Island

Penguin death toll 51. Tasmania positives at 18. New detection in white-fronted tern. Surveillance and collection boosted. Penguin lab results pending confirmation.

South Australia

Mass mortality of 1,000+ greater crested terns on remote islands (Troubridge, North & South Page). Largest state by event count.

Victoria

First confirmed little penguin death (Phillip Island). Vaccination of ~5,000 penguins underway at Phillip Island and St Kilda.

Also active: WA, NSW, QLD. All detections to date remain in wild birds.

What to look for & what to do

Signs in birds
  • • Can’t stand, walk or fly properly
  • • Unusual head/neck posture
  • • Tremors or lack of coordination
  • • Sudden death, especially in groups

Public awareness only — not a diagnosis.

Public action
  • 1. Avoid — do not touch; keep pets away
  • 2. Record — date, time, location, photo
  • 3. Report — 1800 675 888 (24 hr)

Impact layers

Wildlife

Colonial seabirds and little penguins hardest hit so far. Mass die-offs underway.

Tourism

Penguin colonies underpin coastal visitor economies. Closures and losses carry real local cost.

Agriculture

No poultry detections to date. Biosecurity and surveillance remain critical.

Human health

Official risk remains low. Avoid contact with sick or dead birds. Follow Australian CDC advice.
